Our Rust-based Technology

Summitra is a real-time system that uses RUST - BASED WASM for real time posture analysis and feedback.

We alert users of improper feedback using temporal and speed filtering, on the basis of thier OWN custom-defined target thresholds, making our system a real Asana mitraa!

Live Posture Check

Our system looks at your body position in real-time. It maps out specific points on your head and back to catch three common problems:

• Leaning your neck too far forward
• Slouching your back
